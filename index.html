My Journey as a Software Developer at RBC

Abstract

This blog post details my experiences and challenges while working as a Software Developer at the Royal Bank of Canada (RBC). I hope to share valuable insights on professional development, teamwork, and achieving personal goals during my second work term at RBC.

Information about the Employer

I worked as a full stack developer at RBC, creating tools and applications for enterprises within the company. My position was located in Toronto, Ontario, near the GO station. RBC, as one of Canada's largest financial institutions, provided a unique opportunity to work on a range of applications and tools in a fast-paced environment.

Goals

During my secoond work term at RBC, I set several personal and professional goals that I aimed to achieve:

  • Alignment on project objectives and expectations: One of my weaknesses that I expereinced during my first work term was struggling to exactly understand what the client needed from me. For example, the client would give me a requirement, but I would forget or miss little nuances in the requirement and more so focus on the bigger picture. As a result, the final product would miss some of these requirements. To fix this, I ramped up my one on ones with my manager, and also started to have frequent meetings with the client. During these key meetings, I'd ask questions like "Did I miss anything here?" or "Would you like me to change this/is there anything else you are looking for?". I'd also start writing down many more notes. Through these methods, in my second work term I was able to more confidently deliver the clients needs more effectively.
  • Doing more work with a team: While I did work with teams in my first work term, it was at best minimal. In this work term, I wanted to make sure I took full advantage of the opportunities that RBC gave me. At RBC, as an intern, I was given a lot of freedom into choosing how to work on projects that had a team. I could do divide or conquer, or do a much more hands on approach where my team and I would work on problems together. In my first work term, I mostly did the former, working alone even though I had a team. This time around, I took the initiave and constantly looked for opportunities to tackle problems with my team together. To achieve this, I'd be more vocal about how I wanted to be more engaged with my team during team meetings. I also would be more casual with my teammates, creating an environment where we all could trust eachother. Through these methods, I was able to solve problems with my team, rather than alone
  • Managing my time better: At RBC, we are given multiple projects with their own set of requirements rather than just one to focus one. As such, time management is extremely important, as it ensures that I meet deadlines. I achieved this goal by creating a schedule, constantly updating it everyday. On this list, I'd list out requirements for each project, and their respecitve deadlines. I'd make sure to include everything on this schedule so I can easily look to it when I need to. From this simple setup, my time managent skills improved greatly, and I was able to meet every single deadline during my second work term.
  • Stronger Computer Science Skills: During my first work term, I'd sometimes struggle to actually solve the problem at hand. I wanted to improve this, but at first, I wasn't sure how. I tried doing more projects, and more reading, but ultimately, it didnt help me much. What worked for me was using the fact that I had acsess to senior level code/projects. I would start carefully examining these projects, looking for design patterns that I could use in my own work. I also took the initiave to ask my senior developer on to how to tackle common problems in computer science better and more effectively. Through these methods, I was able to greatly enhance my coding skills.
  • Learn new technologies: At RBC, I had the opportunity to choose my own projects. In my first work term, I chose projects that required skills that I was familliar with. In my second, I wanted to expand my horizon, and learn new skills. This meant learning new tools, mainly, React at a more deeper level, along with Redux. To achieve this, I wasn't afraid to ask questions to my teammates, and did a lot of self studying. Through these means, I was able to greatly improve on existing skills, and learned many new ones.

Job Description

At RBC, I worked as a full stack developer, primarily using Django and Express.js. One of my key projects was creating a system-wide reporting system that tracked details about my team's task completion status and other task-related statistics. This project involved utilizing various skills in both backend and frontend development, which I learned both in class and on the job.

Conclusions

Through this experience, I gained valuable insights into professional development, teamwork, and achieving personal goals in a corporate environment. I hope that readers will understand the significance of these lessons and how they contributed to my growth as a software developer.
